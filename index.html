# STOOT – Privacy Policy **Developer:** Wallnight **Contact Email:** waridosan51@gmail.com **Last Updated:** November 23, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Welcome to STOOT, a space-combat game developed by Wallnight. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information is handled when you use STOOT, how it is used, and what third-party services may collect. ## 2. Information Stored on Your Device STOOT stores game progress locally on your device, including: - Coins and scores. - Player levels and progression. - Unlocked ships and upgrades. - Game settings and other gameplay data. This information is stored locally using Android's private application storage. STOOT does not require you to create an account. ## 3. Bluetooth Multiplayer STOOT offers Bluetooth multiplayer functionality. When you use this feature, the game exchanges necessary gameplay information directly between connected devices. This may include: - Game mode and ship selection. - Player readiness and control inputs. - Multiplayer game state. - Device model information. Bluetooth permissions are requested to enable multiplayer functionality. STOOT does not use these permissions to track your GPS location. ## 4. Advertising and Google AdMob STOOT uses Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect certain information through the advertising SDK, including: - IP address. - Advertising ID and other device identifiers. - App and advertising interactions. - Diagnostic and performance information. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and service functionality. Advertising and consent management are handled through Google's advertising and consent technologies. For more information, please review: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) [Google Mobile Ads Privacy Information](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure) ## 5. Advertising Choices and Consent STOOT uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable privacy consent requirements. Depending on your location and applicable regulations, you may be presented with a consent form or privacy choices. Rewarded advertisements are optional. You can continue playing without watching an advertisement. ## 6. Data Sharing STOOT does not operate its own game account system or game server. Game progress is stored locally on your device. Multiplayer gameplay information is exchanged directly with the connected Bluetooth player. Information collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK may be processed or shared by Google and its advertising partners as described in Google's privacy documentation. ## 7. Data Security and Retention Game progress remains stored on your device until it is deleted through available game or device controls. Information handled by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's applicable data retention and privacy practices. We do not operate a separate STOOT account database. ## 8. Children's Privacy STOOT is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Advertising and audience settings are subject to the applicable Google Play and advertising policies. If you have concerns about children's privacy, please contact us. ## 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when STOOT's features, services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page, with its updated date. ## 10.
