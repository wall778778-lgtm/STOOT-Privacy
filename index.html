Privacy Policy

01 Introduction

STOOT is a space-combat game developed by Wallnight. This policy describes information handled by the Android game and the Google advertising technology it uses.

STOOT does not require an account. It does not operate a game server or cloud-save service.

02 Information stored on your device

STOOT stores game progress in Android private app storage using SharedPreferences. The saved progress includes:

  • Coins, level, experience, and best score.
  • Current and maximum health.
  • Selected and unlocked ships.
  • World coordinates, ore, cells, and laser-resistance upgrades.

The app also stores a small permission-request flag so it can avoid repeatedly prompting for Bluetooth permissions.

This information is used to save and restore local game progress and permission-prompt state. STOOT does not save separate game-settings preferences in its current Android implementation.

03 Bluetooth multiplayer

If you choose Bluetooth Play, STOOT discovers nearby devices and connects directly to another player's phone. The game exchanges multiplayer information such as game mode, ship selection, readiness, control input, and game state. The connection message includes the Android device model name.

Bluetooth device information is used in memory to list and connect to devices. Multiplayer information is sent directly to the connected player; it is not sent to a STOOT-operated server.

STOOT requests Bluetooth permissions for this feature. On Android 10 and 11, Android requires location permission for Bluetooth discovery. STOOT does not read GPS coordinates or use that permission to track your location.

04 Advertising and Google AdMob

STOOT offers an optional Google Mobile Ads rewarded video after a solo run ends. Watching is your choice; you can retry or return to the menu without watching. Completing the ad grants one hit point and a temporary 2.5-second shield. STOOT does not show banner or interstitial ads during gameplay.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google data such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App and ad interactions, including app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as launch time, hang rate, and energy use.
  • Device and account identifiers, including Android Advertising ID and App Set ID and, where applicable, other account-related identifiers.

Google identifies advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention as purposes for this SDK data and states that it is encrypted in transit using TLS. Advertising ID collection is optional and can be restricted through Android settings or supported SDK privacy features. Google’s and ad partners’ own privacy terms govern their handling and retention of advertising data.

STOOT uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to refresh consent information and show consent forms when required. Whether an ad can be requested depends on UMP’s consent state and the privacy choices configured for this app. When UMP requires it, the in-game Privacy menu provides a way to revisit privacy choices. Ads may be unavailable.

Debug versions use Google test ads; release versions use STOOT’s AdMob ad placement.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · Google Mobile Ads data disclosure

05 Data sharing and services

STOOT does not have accounts, cloud saves, or its own game server. Game progress stays in the app’s private storage. Multiplayer data is exchanged directly with the Bluetooth player you connect to.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK communicates with Google and may share the advertising-related data described above with Google and parties involved in ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention.

The current Android source does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

06 Data security, retention, and deletion

STOOT’s saved progress is kept in Android private app storage on your device. STOOT does not save Bluetooth session details as a player profile. Google handles advertising-related data under its own applicable privacy and retention practices.

To delete STOOT’s locally saved data: open Android Settings, find STOOT under Apps, open Storage, and choose Clear storage or Clear data. Uninstalling STOOT also removes its local app data. These actions may permanently remove your saved progress.

STOOT has no in-game account or data-deletion feature because it does not operate user accounts or store game progress on a STOOT server.

07 Children and audience

STOOT does not ask for a player’s age. The developer declares the intended age groups in Google Play Console. If children are included in the target audience, additional Google Play Families and child-directed advertising requirements may apply. Advertising and audience settings must be configured for the declared audience.
